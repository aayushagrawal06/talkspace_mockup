Design notes: what changed and why

Benchmarked against Psychology Today (weighted most), Grow Therapy, Talkiatry, and Rula. Use the mockup tools at the bottom of the page to turn on Annotations and Template slots.

Added from competitors (gaps in Talkspace)

  • Psychology TodayConcern + place in the H1, real breadcrumbs. The live template has “Therapists in [city/state]” and a placeholder crumb. With four concern pages, naming the concern confirms the visitor is in the right place.
  • GrowRulaIntro line with live count, $0 copay, and match speed. Answers “is this for me, and can I afford it?” before the first card.
  • Grow“Availability updated” line. A cheap trust signal; needs a data timestamp.
  • Psychology TodayProfile pop-up (name or “View profile”). Follows PT’s profile order: verified header, “Let’s connect” card, About, Practice at a glance, Insurance, Qualifications, Specialties and expertise, Client focus, Treatment approach, Location. Opens over the results so people keep their filters and place.
  • RulaTalkspaceReworked results sidebar. One panel instead of three loose boxes: match CTA with Talkspace’s real timelines, a live summary of the current results (accepting, psychiatry, in-network), the plans Talkspace works with, a concern-aware self-check link, and a 988 strip (plus SAMHSA on Addiction). It shows results; it adds no new filter controls.
  • TalkspaceFAQ answers in dropdowns, as on the live page: one open at a time, first open by default, +/– toggles, no motion. All 8 questions are concern + location specific and respond to the filters. Per-concern safety content (988, SAMHSA, withdrawal warning) lives here and in the sidebar.
  • GrowPsychology TodayFour-part SEO content block above the FAQ: what the condition is, what Talkspace provides in the place (live counts by service, age and plan), how Talkspace helps with recovery, and symptoms with precautions. National prevalence figures for anxiety, depression and addiction are cited to NIMH and SAMHSA; Georgia’s crisis line (GCAL) is cited to the state; other states fall back to 988 until verified lines are added to the STATE_RES table. No testimonials: no genuine, condition-specific ones were available.
  • Psychology TodayWhole page responds to filters. H1, breadcrumbs, intro, sidebar stats, explore heading, location heading and FAQs all update from the Concern filter; the other filters feed the intro, stats and FAQ answers.
  • TalkspaceCities and states merged into one “Find [concern] therapists in other places” block with two tabs.
  • Removed“About [concern] therapy” block, on request.
  • Psychology TodayGrowRow cards (identity left, details middle, action right). Easier to scan and compare bios than stacked tiles.
  • GrowConcern chips with +N, and the concern you filtered by highlighted. Shows why each provider matched.
  • Talkiatry“How I work” and “Your first session” on expand. Lowers the fear of the first appointment without lengthening every card.
  • Rula“In network with [plan]” on the card when an insurance filter is set. Rula leads with insurance; this applies the idea at card level.
  • Psychology Today“Licensed in [state]” line. A light version of PT’s Verified / Endorsed signals. Talkspace cards have no equivalent today.
  • RulaThree numbered steps in the get-started band. The steps are a true sequence, so numbering is justified.

Kept because Talkspace’s version is stronger

  • Talkspace“Not accepting” state routes to a match. PT shows a waitlist label and dead-ends; Grow only lists available people.
  • TalkspaceTherapy / Psychiatry tags show integrated care at a glance.
  • TalkspaceCheck availability as the primary action, instead of PT’s phone and email buttons. Keeps the booking inside the platform.
  • TalkspaceExplore-by hub (concern, insurance, therapy type, age), popular cities, and state links. PT only cross-links nearby cities.
  • TalkspaceInsurance promo bar, cost and start-time answers, 988 notice. The 988 notice now also sits beside results.
  • TalkspaceEmpty-state wording and the “Ready to get started?” CTA copy.

Considered, not adopted

  • SkippedMap / list toggle (PT, Grow). Care is online and licensed by state, so a map implies a proximity that doesn’t matter here.
  • SkippedPT’s quick toggles and 12-category filter drawer. You asked for the filter bar to stay exactly as is.
  • Added on requestPhone and email on each card (Psychology Today shows both). Check availability stays the primary action. Direct contact can pull conversations and scheduling outside the platform, so confirm with the product team that Talkspace wants clinicians' details exposed. All values here are fictional.
  • SkippedPT profile items that assume a private practice: per-session fee and sliding scale, peer endorsements, personal website, street address with map, and the free-consultation call. Talkspace is online and insurance-based. “Check availability” is the primary action instead.
  • SkippedStar ratings and personality tags (Grow). The live page shows no rating or style data. Can be added later as optional card slots.
  • Moved“What is Talkspace’s address in my area?” FAQ. The address is the corporate mailing address; it now lives in the footer.

Assumptions to verify

  • Palette: brand teal #005c65, pale teal #e1f0ee, ink #2e0404 and the filter-bar colors are sampled from your screenshots. Trust Teal, Pink, Green, Orange, Blue, Lemon and Maroon are still approximations; swap them in the :root tokens.
  • Filter option lists (Type, Age, Service, Concern) are inferred; only the closed pills were visible in the screenshot. Pill shape, order, labels, and “Clear filters” are replicated. The grey “Clear filters” text is about 2.8:1 on white, left as shown.
  • Logo and FAQ illustration are vector traces of your screenshots. Replace them with Talkspace’s original SVG files.
  • Photos and all provider data (including phone numbers and emails) are placeholders.
